ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Fareeha, an Illustrator and an Animator, passionate about bringing life to visual stories.
I want to experience every aspect of art I while I have the chance.
Rather than sticking to one art form, I'm constantly experimenting from watercolors and acrylics to digital illustration, animations.

With these different flavours of medium, I'm interested not only in the mediums themselves, but in the technicalities behind them like composition, colour, texture, timing, and visual storytelling.
I therfore show you my Frankenstiens...
